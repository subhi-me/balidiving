Snorkeling Tours

Go on one of our fantastic snorkeling adventures and choose between snorkelling on coral bommies and white sand bottoms, the shipwreck of a WW2 transport ship, or cavorting with the magical Manta Rays at Nusa Penida. Snorkelling is an activity anyone can do…..even non-swimmers

Select Package

If you're not into Scuba Diving, then there are many other options for you to explore the underwater world of Bali, the main one is snorkeling. We have chosen some of the best Bali snorkeling sites for you to be able to enjoy and explore the wonders of the underwater world, the visibility will be amazing, the marine life and corals even better. bring your camera or rent one from us, to make sure that you capture all of the wonderful memories you will gather during your trip with Bali Diving.

Snorkeling is an activity that virtually anyone who is reasonably healthy, can participate in. From 8 to 80 or even outside these ages, literally anyone can experience the absolute beauty that lives below the surface of the waters surrounding Bali. It's literally an activity the whole family can do, and of course…… it's affordable! Bali Diving takes you to the same sites we take our divers, so you can see underwater Bali at its very best. Everything is included in our packages – bottled water, experienced dive guides, use of all equipment and a sumptuous lunch. Become our friend on Facebook and we’ll tag you in any photos as a memento of your experience

You will be provided with your own guide who will be able to show you over the reefs and make sure you have a fantastic time. Some of the Bali snorkeling locations include Tulamben, Padang Bai, and Nusa Penida. Have a look at our snorkel locations below to see what you could be enjoying, when you come snorkeling in Bali with Bali Diving. If there are any special locations that you would like to visit that we have not listed then please do get in touch as we have access to the whole of Bali. We also offer Snorkeling tours where we can combine for you over a period of days and group size, drop us a line to find out more.
