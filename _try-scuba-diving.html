Try Scuba Diving

If you do not have a diving certification or have never dived before we can take you diving at this site on a "Try Scuba Diving Program". Its still two dives, the same as qualified divers,but you will be supervised directly by a qualified Instructor at a maximum ratio of 2 divers per guide when they take you diving and limited to a maximum diving depth of 12 metres. 

Select Package

This diving program includes two ocean dives for participants who are comfortable in the water and medically fit. The program starts with a briefing on basic diving skills, signals, and safety, followed by an introduction to scuba skills in shallow water. The dives are led by a PADI qualified dive leader with close supervision, and the maximum ratio of divers to professionals is 4:1, although we prefer to keep the ratio at 2:1 for extra safety.
