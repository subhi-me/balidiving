Ask us

The Padi Open Water Diver Referral Bali course is made for you if you have already completed a part of the PADI Open Water course with a PADI Instructor (for example the theory part and all Confined Water sessions in the pool) and you want to complete your certification. The PADI Open Water Diver Bali certification allows you to dive to a maximum depth of 18 metres under the supervision of a dive guide (or autonomously with a dive buddy, according to local law). This certification is recognized all over the world.
