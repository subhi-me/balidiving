Discover Scuba Diving

Discover Scuba Diving in Bali "The Island of the Gods", is certainly a Scuba Diving and diving, you will explore underwater beauty. Indonesian dive sites in Bali, Indonesia are exceptional.

We could be diving on the incredible USAT Liberty Wreck in Tulamben, or Exploring the deep dropoff of Nusa Penida and Crystal Bay where you could get the chance to spot the Oceanic Sunfish (Mola Mola) or go diving in Nusa Penida with Manta Ray's.

We also have some of the best Muck diving for you to take your underwater camera, we are at Bali Diving, you have lots of hard and soft corals.

The USAT Liberty Wreck has been in the top 15 dive sites in the world and still there as one of them.
